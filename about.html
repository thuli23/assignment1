Our website, FindMyBaker, addreeses a growing market needs: connecting consumers with affordable, quality bakers
while giving small bakery businesses greater exposure. The platform will serve as a marketplace that showcases bakeries
and helps customers easily locate their preferred baker at a reasonable prices. The primary goal is to link consumers with their favorite bakers,
fostering loyalty. The specific objectives are to provide a showcase for bakers to advertise their products
and services. It also increases website traffic by attracting consumer attention through an intuitive search and discovery
experience. Lastly it empowers small bakeries with limited marketing resources to gain visibility and grow their customer base.\

Our main target audience is mainly the bakers and those looking into ordering online from a bakery.
